# Product Brief

## Purpose

Pegasus Dreamscapes Website / MarketFlow is the public operating front door for Pegasus Dreamscapes. It should make Pegasus feel credible, safe, systemized, and ready to review complex real estate situations without exposing unnecessary internal workflow detail.

## Audience

- Motivated, distressed, or complex-property sellers.
- Agents and industry professionals checking credibility.
- Investor-buyers and capital/development partners.
- Wholesalers, deal finders, and source-protection relationships.
- People scanning a QR/business card route before deciding whether to submit a property.

## Core Workflow

1. Visitor brings a property or situation.
2. Pegasus captures context through `/bring-an-opportunity`, Strategy Lab, Peggy, `/connect`, or MarketFlow request access.
3. Pegasus reviews and underwrites.
4. Pegasus structures the appropriate participation lane.
5. A human-reviewed next step is offered only when the situation fits.

## Launch Promise

The site can explain Pegasus, capture serious opportunities, route visitors to the right door, and preserve compliance boundaries. It must not pretend that private dashboards, inventory, automated offers, public investing, or paid checkout flows are live when they are not.

## Not Yet

Do not launch fake status tracking, fixture library content as real insight, public Deal Blueprint checkout, public MarketFlow deal inventory, Peggy phone/voice intake, or public investment solicitation language.
