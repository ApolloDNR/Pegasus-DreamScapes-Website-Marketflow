# Pegasus Redesign Brief — "The Floor" Direction

Prepared July 12, 2026 · for Apollo · by Claude

## 1. What you said

The site is overwhelming and hard to navigate. Strategy Lab opens on a cockpit and confuses instead of inviting. The whole thing needs better storytelling, better text, better illustration — and you want the departments to be an experience: walking the halls, exploring each department. You're right on every count, and here's the plan.

## 2. Diagnosis (why it feels that way)

The site *passes QA* but *fails feel*, for three measurable reasons:

- **Too many front doors.** The nav exposes ~15 routes across two dropdown groups. A first-time visitor is asked to make a taxonomy decision ("Who We Serve vs What We Do?") before they understand what Pegasus is.
- **The homepage tries to say everything.** It's ~8 full screens deep and every section carries equal visual weight — so nothing leads. That's the "overwhelming" you're feeling: not too much design, too little hierarchy.
- **Strategy Lab greets with instruments, not a question.** The cockpit is genuinely powerful, but power shown first reads as work. Tools should be discovered, not confronted.

## 3. The organizing idea: the site is a building

Pegasus is an operating company with a floor of departments — so the site should feel like **visiting that floor**. One metaphor, carried everywhere:

| Visit moment | Site surface | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Arrive** (lobby) | Homepage | One thesis, one router, one proof. Calm. |
| **Walk** (the hall) | Departments → **The Floor** | Cinematic walk; four doors; explore each room |
| **Sit down** (the desk) | Strategy Lab | One question on one desk; instruments in a drawer |
| **Leave something** (front desk) | Submit a Property | The intake desk — already good, keep |

This resolves "overwhelming" without deleting substance: depth moves **behind doors** instead of stacking on one page.

## 4. The four moves

**A. Navigation → five items.**
`Start · The Floor · Strategy Lab · Work With Apollo · [Submit a Property]` + the Peggy concierge dock. Everything else (sellers/buyers/dealfinders/capital/operators/referral, library, projects, legal) stays reachable from the footer, from Peggy, and from contextual links inside the walk — not from the top bar. Lanes get *routed to*, not *browsed to*.

**B. Homepage → a lobby (5 beats, ~3 screens).**
1. Thesis hero (one line, one dusk plate) 2. "What brings you here?" router (keep — it's your best section) 3. One proof (Nelson, one stat trio) 4. The doorway: "Walk the floor →" (leads into the 3D hall) 5. Submit CTA + footer. Everything else on today's homepage either moves into a department room or gets cut.

**C. Strategy Lab → "One desk, one question."**
Opens on a single centered prompt — *"What's the property, and what's weighing on it?"* — set on a beautiful desk surface (same warm-dusk art direction). You answer, it gives the Quick Read. Then, and only then, a copper drawer handle: **"Open the instruments"** slides the full cockpit up for people who want the numbers. Iconic, single-focus, zero spread. (All existing compliance copy, tier strip, and run-limit logic stays — this is re-choreography, not rebuild.)

**D. A real illustration system.**
The style-locked Higgsfield plates (warm-dusk, navy/copper, no people) become the site's visual language: corridor, four department rooms (done today), plus a lobby plate and an intake-desk plate. One grade, one world — this kills the "sparse and texty" feel with imagery that's unmistakably yours, generated from your own corridor reference so it never looks like stock or generic AI gloss.

## 5. What's live right now

**The Floor — playable prototype: https://apollodnr.github.io/hall/**
Scroll = walk the dusk corridor. Four copper-framed doors — Acquisitions, Development, Dispositions, Asset Management — light up as you approach; tap one to step into its room (a Higgsfield plate styled from your corridor reference) with the department's real copy and a link deeper. Story beats fade in along the walk. Ends at the vault: *"One property. Four departments. One routed path."* → Submit. Reduced-motion and older phones get a flat editorial fallback. ("Skip the walk" up top does the same.)

Read it as **direction, not final**: plates are v1 (regenerate-able in minutes), motion values are first-pass, and the numeral plaques need a polish pass.
