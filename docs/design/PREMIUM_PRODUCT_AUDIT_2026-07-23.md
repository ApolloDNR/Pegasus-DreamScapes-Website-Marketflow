# Pegasus Premium Product Audit — 2026-07-23

## Mandate

Preserve the approved architectural/editorial homepage language while making the wider product feel established, reachable, and professionally governed. The immediate owner concerns were discoverability, Strategy Lab quality, MarketFlow visibility, and the absence of a coherent “More” directory.

## Product verdict before this pass

The premium public spine was strong, but the navigation understated the breadth of the firm. Strategy Lab carried two competing interfaces and invented fit scores that read like an older AI prototype. MarketFlow existed in the route map but did not announce itself as a first-class product. The result was a site that looked mature at arrival yet felt less mature as soon as a visitor tried to understand the full operating system.

## Design principles applied

1. **Preserve the approved arrival.** The homepage hero, typography, Hellenic-modern imagery, documentary proof, navy, limestone, and restrained bronze remain the governing visual language.
2. **Make product depth visible.** Strategy Lab and MarketFlow are first-class navigation destinations. A deliberate More directory exposes company, proof, operating lanes, systems, and resources without overcrowding the primary bar.
3. **Replace “AI score” theater with decision records.** Strategy Lab now organizes property context, basis, objectives, real modeled economics, executable paths, open questions, and a saved decision brief. It does not manufacture a 0–100 fit score.
4. **Treat MarketFlow as a governed relationship layer.** The public product explains roles, permissions, review gates, source protection, written terms, and what remains private. It does not present public inventory or claim automatic matching.
5. **Keep boundaries legible.** Directional tools, human diligence, licensed representation, private capital relationships, and controlled-pilot access are distinguished in the interface—not buried in fine print.
6. **Design for professional scrutiny.** Desktop, mobile, light, dark, keyboard, reduced-motion, and small-text contrast were reviewed as product states.

## Changes completed

### Global navigation

- Added persistent Strategy Lab and MarketFlow destinations.
- Added an accessible More mega-directory with three clear groups:
  - Company & proof
  - Operating lanes
  - Network & resources
- Reordered mobile navigation around the primary opportunity desk, Strategy Lab, MarketFlow, and the full site directory.
- Preserved Escape, outside-click, current-page, focus-return, and modal focus-trap behavior.

### Strategy Lab

- Replaced the shallow welcome/cockpit stack with one premium four-step working desk:
  1. Property
  2. Basis
  3. Strategy
  4. Review
- Added grounded calculations for basis, modeled carry, exit costs, net proceeds, spread, and margin on modeled cost.
- Added scenario ordering, open-question disclosure, browser-local saving, Peggy handoff, and canonical Property Read routing.
- Preserved the documented calculator deep link.
- Removed invented fit-score presentation and any implication of a valuation, offer, or automated professional judgment.

### MarketFlow

- Rebuilt the public page as a controlled private-pilot product.
- Added role-specific relationship briefs for deal sources, buyers, capital, and operators.
- Added the gated relationship sequence:
  Strategy Lab → Pegasus review → approved record → considered introduction → written terms.
- Added an anonymized dossier preview that demonstrates the product without publishing live inventory or private terms.
- Linked the public access desk and public criteria ledger directly from the product.

## Verification evidence

The rebuilt product surfaces were rendered in an isolated browser prototype at desktop and phone sizes before source assembly.

- TypeScript contracts: passed for the rebuilt Strategy Lab, MarketFlow, and navigation components.
- Syntax diagnostics: clean for the integrated page and navigation source.
- Browser errors: none attributable to the product surfaces.
- Horizontal overflow: zero at 1440px and 390px.
- Broken product images: zero in the rendered prototype.
- H1 count: one per product surface.
- Strategy Lab step navigation, data entry, review brief, and browser save: passed.
- MarketFlow role switching: passed.
- WCAG contrast audit: zero color-contrast violations in light and dark product states.
- Reduced-motion overrides and visible focus states are included.

The isolated prototype shell itself omitted the production document title, language attribute, and main landmark; the real Pegasus shell already supplies those global document contracts. Those prototype-only omissions are not part of the committed components.

## Remaining hard gate

The public MarketFlow story is now premium and reachable. The authenticated MarketFlow member/operator application is a separate product surface and still requires a dedicated page-by-page design, permissions, privacy, and workflow audit before it should be described as finished. That work should begin with the role router, dashboard, deal records, offer studio, messaging, and admin review surfaces—not with another cosmetic landing-page pass.

## Next recommended design sequence

1. Audit the authenticated MarketFlow role router and dashboard against the public relationship model.
2. Redesign deal records and offer studio around documented decisions, permissions, and source history.
3. Unify access, buybox interest, and member onboarding language.
4. Review secondary public pages in the More directory for one clear job and one signature moment each.
5. Update the isolated live preview only after the full repository build and route suite are rerun on a stable workspace.
