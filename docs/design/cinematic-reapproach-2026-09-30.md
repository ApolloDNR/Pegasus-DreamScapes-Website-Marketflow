# Cinematic reapproach

Apollo rejected the framed hero and requested a cinematic, intentionally designed experience across every page. This direction supersedes the earlier parchment hero treatment, while retaining approved copy, original assets, real facts, consent boundaries, and the six-section Home architecture.

## Visual system

- Paper `#f5efe4`, midnight `#0b1d29`, ivory `#fcfaf6`, copper `#8e5030`, pale copper `#d9a47c`.
- Playfair Display for narrative headings; Inter for body copy, navigation, forms, and controls. No negative tracking.
- 1280px reading grid, 64px desktop gutters, 40px tablet, 24px phone. Headings scale by context; controls retain a 44px minimum target.
- Native rectangular buttons, underlined links, thin chapter rules, open editorial rows. No ornamental card grid.
- Light navigation leads into the original full-bleed East Bay image. A local navy scrim protects text; the image is not washed out. Theme changes never substitute photographs or change crop geometry.
- All photographs remain original files. Image-generation concepts are layout references, not replacement evidence. Supplied factual copy takes precedence over generated spelling, invented copy, or illustrative substitutions.

## Home sequence

1. Arrival: full-width bay and colonnade, large two-line promise, two existing actions, truthful architectural-vision caption, native link into visitor paths.
2. Orientation: paper, original three-threshold illustration, three direct routes in a ruled directory. Existing labels, notes, and links remain authoritative.
3. Evidence: navy, large unaltered Nelson after photograph paired with the smaller original before photograph. Local buttons explain three actual renovation details. The full record is one link away.
4. Founder: original Apollo portrait, oversized name, brief background, a clear separation for licensed representation.
5. Planning: an open desk with eight questions and live local feedback. Existing calculations and destinations remain intact.
6. Invitation: return to the opening environment, concise request, explicit submission boundary, existing before-you-begin disclosure.

## Motion

Only scene imagery moves: a single restrained camera settle and at most 28px of native scroll depth. No scroll hijacking, pinned reading sequence, delayed text, hidden controls, autoplay audio, or perpetual rendering loop. Work pauses offscreen and when the document is hidden. Reduced-motion mode removes the movement completely. Hover and selection feedback explain real interactive state.

## Concept references

Six fresh standalone concepts cover arrival, orientation, project, founder, planning, and invitation. Implementation uses original assets and actual copy even where generated references approximate those details. Screenshots are compared at desktop and phone sizes before handoff. Every public route and relevant status route is included in the browser review matrix.
